## 🇿🇦Serenity🇿🇦

Serenity is the original deal: getting VivaChek drug/screening test kits into the South African market, with Dr. Kudzai Nyamuomba sourcing kits (via supplier Frank) and Nicole Chitareni handling local compliance and logistics out of Louis Trichardt, trading as MedSerenity. By mid-September the regulatory picture had sharpened considerably. Norman Nyamuwomba, added to the group 9/8 to chase SAHPRA directly, visited the regulator's Cape Town office in person on 9/18 — only to learn that office handles Radiation Control exclusively and has nothing to do with test-kit licensing. He was redirected to SAHPRA's Operations Control section online, with the real head office in Pretoria and a secondary office in Durban.

That redirect turned out to be the useful one. Dr. Kudzai emailed SAHPRA directly, Norman followed up by phone, and on 9/25 SAHPRA sent back the actual, official requirements for a Medical Devices establishment licence: a cover letter addressed to SAHPRA's CEO, a signed and initialled licence application (Annexure 1), a completed Excel application form (Annexure 2), proof of payment of R15,600 (Annexure 3), a CV for the Authorised Representative (Annexure 4), and a Quality Manual (Annexure 5) — all emailed to mdadmin@sahpra.org.za, with a stated 6–8 week evaluation window. This superseded the earlier, vaguer vendor quote from Pharmers (which had priced the same application stage at R15,600 but bundled with consulting and collection fees the team had already decided to skip).

Since then, progress has been mostly about getting everyone on a call to actually divide up and assemble the application. A group call on 10/3 was missed; Nicole was traveling and offline through 10/5–10/6; and by 10/13 the team had settled on holding the planning call after work hours, since Norman — who works days — is only free from 5:30pm on weekdays or anytime on weekends. As of this sync, that call hadn't yet been confirmed held, and no part of the application had been compiled or submitted. The original courier shipment of 5 test kits to Nicole, delayed back in August by her bout of flu, also still has no confirmed delivery in the chat.

**Status: In progress — official SAHPRA requirements in hand since 9/25; blocked on scheduling the team call needed to divide up and submit the 5-annexure application.**
